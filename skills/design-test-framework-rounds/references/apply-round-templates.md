# Apply-round templates

## 1. Authority and state classes

| Code | Meaning | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| REQ | Sourced requirement | Cite the source and locator; preserve confirmation status |
| DER | Agent derivation | Keep separate from sourced facts |
| DIR | Human design direction | Applies to the authorized design round; does not become a client or statutory requirement |
| ASM | Design assumption | Label the premise and its confirmation or expiry condition |
| OQ | Open question | Keep open until sufficient evidence or authority closes it |
| CONFLICT | Source conflict | Preserve both claims; do not choose silently |

Human feedback must be recorded before it is interpreted. A design direction is not automatically a client requirement, planning condition, or project fact.

## 2. Test-framework template: 15 fields

1. Framework name
2. Project tension or sub-question
3. Directly related requirement IDs and locators
4. Eligible method cards and evidence boundary
5. Translation from evidence to project-specific question
6. Adjustable variables
7. Spatial and operational implications
8. Organizing mechanism
9. Work that can proceed with current evidence
10. Work blocked by missing evidence
11. Failure conditions
12. Initial cost, code, structure, operations, and maintenance risks
13. Minimum validation model
14. Human decision required
15. Evidence status and assumption declaration

## 3. Comparison matrix

Compare: project-evidence coverage | response to the tension | dependency on missing inputs | operational explainability | code/structure/cost exposure | risk of copying source form | current falsifiability.

Recommend only a test order. Do not select the final design.

## 4. Minimum-model task card

Record: purpose | permitted inputs | output | suggested representation | prohibited representation | falsifiable completion test | stop condition | human decision.

Suggested representations include program blocks, relationship matrices, circulation layers, abstract massing relationships, or operational sequences. They must not masquerade as a real site plan.

## 5. Test status

- `PASS` — the stated logical test passes with identified evidence.
- `PARTIAL` — part of the mechanism is supported; list missing evidence.
- `BLOCKED` — the test cannot proceed without named evidence, authority, or professional input.

An idealized operating condition may be tested only when explicitly authorized and must remain a labelled design assumption.

## 6. Versioning and files

- Use the project-defined output and log directories.
- Create a new version unless overwrite is explicitly authorized.
- Preserve failed baselines as regression evidence.
- Re-read written files and report every touched path.
- Keep intermediate OCR, rendering, or extraction files in a clearly marked working area; they are not evidence until verified against the source.

## 7. Historical or unverified site material

Register provenance, author or role, date, confirmation status, and current-validity status. Text visible in a source image may be recorded as `source observation`; it does not automatically become a current planning fact. Preserve inconsistencies in names, symbols, boundaries, or dimensions and create an open question rather than silently correcting them.
